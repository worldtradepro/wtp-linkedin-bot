This week
Awards rose by six to 56 this week while new tenders fell by four to 30, so more work is being booked than put out to bid. China Communications Construction (CCCC) took three of those awards, led by the US$864m Annaba Port facilities contract in Algeria. For bidders, the open windows worth working are in European grid equipment: Západoslovenská distribučná wants three-phase oil power transformers by 4 December, and Skellefteå Kraft wants 400/130kV units by 16 January.
— World Trade Pro Research

Deals of the week

1 / 6
Annaba Port facilities construction, Algeria
■ Awarded  ·  🇩🇿 Algeria  ·  US$864m  ·  China Communications Construction (CCCC)
CCCC is now booked on a US$864m Algerian port job, so marine works suppliers should approach it as a buyer rather than compete for the main contract.
2 / 6
At Tullkammarkajen
■ Tender  ·  🇸🇪 Sweden  ·  US$51m  ·  bids due 18 Dec  ·  buyer Halmstads Kommun
Halmstads Kommun is putting about US$51m into quay works, with bids due 18 December, a sizeable municipal port job for marine contractors.
3 / 6
Three-phase oil power transformers VVN/VN - joint procurement - PCI
■ Tender  ·  🇸🇰 Slovakia  ·  US$42m  ·  bids due 4 Dec  ·  buyer Západoslovenská distribučná, a.s.
A joint procurement worth about US$42m shows a Slovak grid operator buying transformers in volume; makers have until 4 December to bid.
4 / 6
Ships for Marine Fisheries Monitoring
■ Tender  ·  🇭🇷 Croatia  ·  US$36m  ·  bids due 19 Nov  ·  buyer Ministarstvo Poljoprivrede, Šumarstva I Ribarstva
Croatia's agriculture ministry is buying fisheries monitoring vessels worth about US$36m, and shipyards have a short window that closes on 19 November.
5 / 6
400/130kV transformers PT2 Lidmyran
■ Tender  ·  🇸🇪 Sweden  ·  US$25m  ·  bids due 16 Jan  ·  buyer Skellefteå Kraft AB
Skellefteå Kraft is buying 400/130kV transformers worth about US$25m, and the deadline of 16 January gives makers time to price carefully.

Shenzhen Shawan River reservoir water quality assurance project phase II, Shenzhen
■ Awarded  ·  🇨🇳 China  ·  US$69m  ·  China Communications Construction (CCCC)
