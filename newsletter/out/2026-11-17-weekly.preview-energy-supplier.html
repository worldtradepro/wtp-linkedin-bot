This week
Awards rose to 76 this week from 58, and EPC items to 142 from 117, but the money went to a few hands. MCC took the two largest metals awards: the US$195m Vinmetal steelmaking package in Vietnam and US$120m of bauxite mining works at Sanmenxia. Voith Hydro won both Slovak pump-turbine and motor-generator packages, and Alstom Ferroviaria took Italian signalling. For a bidder, the pattern is that head contracts are going to integrated contractors and equipment makers. The realistic opening is a subcontract or supply position under them, not the main contract.
