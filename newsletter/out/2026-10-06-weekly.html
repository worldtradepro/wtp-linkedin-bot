This week
Europe did the buying this week. Tenders rose from 5 to 30 on the week, nearly all of them public notices from Poland, the UK, Ireland and the Balkans, and the biggest award was Romania's US$2.2bn Cernavodă nuclear contract, which went to a Canadian-Italian-Korean consortium; the five largest awards went to Canadian, Irish, British and Indian contractors. If you sell into European power and rail, the bid calendar below is your next fortnight; if you buy, the award values are your benchmarks. On the water, Hormuz crude is back to pre-war volumes while Venezuela's barrels are being priced out by freight.
— Yi Huang, World Trade Pro
