Oil, gas & LNG
🇻🇪 Venezuela’s Oil Exports Drop 9% as Freight Costs Bite
oilprice.com
Venezuela’s oil exports fell nearly 9% in September to 1.08 million barrels per day as soaring tanker costs forced traders to demand steeper discounts and delayed cargoes leaving the country.
What it means: Less is moving from this origin: crude, LNG and product buyers can expect firmer offers and longer lead times for a few weeks.

🇵🇦 New limits: More ships can transit this key cargo chokepoint
freightwaves.com
The Panama Canal has raised the number of daily transit slots for the largest container vessels.
What it means: The Panama Canal carries Atlantic → Pacific (US Gulf LNG & grain → Asia) and logged 1 critical signal this week. More capacity on this route: shippers and forwarders can expect shorter queues and softer slot premiums, usually within weeks.
