This week
Awards rose to 52 this week from 35, and EPC items rose to 98 from 78. The largest open tickets are in European rail and ports: Romania's CFR wants bids by 23 Nov on a US$864m, 7-lot track superstructure tender, and Dublin Port has put out a US$280m civil works framework. MCC took two Chinese EPC awards, a US$175m blast furnace and a US$143m limestone mine. For bidders, the pattern is public owners in Europe bundling maintenance and civil work into lots and frameworks, so line up partners and suppliers before the deadlines, not after.
— World Trade Pro Research

Deals of the week

1 / 6
Railway maintenance works - Replacement of components of the track superstructure - 7 lots
■ Tender  ·  🇷🇴 Romania  ·  US$864m  ·  bids due 23 Nov  ·  buyer Compania Nationala de Cai Ferate "CFR" - SA
Seven lots let mid-sized track contractors and rail component suppliers bid for a share of US$864m, and the 23 Nov deadline leaves about five weeks to price.
2 / 6
Electrification Rurale 2027-2030
■ Tender  ·  🇫🇷 France  ·  US$20m  ·  bids due 12 Nov  ·  buyer SDEE de la Lozère
A local syndicate is buying four years of rural electrification for US$20m: steady, small-ticket work for regional line contractors and network equipment suppliers until 2030.
3 / 6
Multi-Party Framework Agreement for Civil Engineering Works at Dublin Port
■ Tender  ·  🇮🇪 Ireland  ·  US$280m  ·  buyer Dublin Port Company_411
A multi-party framework means several contractors will share US$280m of port civil works, so getting onto the panel matters more than winning any single job.
4 / 6
Fujian Dadonghai high-end steel industry project 2×1200m³ blast furnace construction
■ Awarded  ·  🇨🇳 China  ·  US$175m  ·  Metallurgical Corporation of China (MCC)
Fujian Dadonghai is adding blast furnace capacity and has booked MCC as EPC, so furnace equipment and refractory suppliers should pitch to MCC, not the owner.
5 / 6
Ruyang County Yude Mining limestone mining for cement project
■ Awarded  ·  🇨🇳 China  ·  US$143m  ·  Metallurgical Corporation of China (MCC)
A US$143m EPC for a limestone mine that feeds cement shows MCC taking mining work outside steel, so equipment vendors should treat MCC as the buyer.

Dams and similar structures - AM MULTISERVICIO OBRA CIVIL HYDRO IBERIA (2026 - 2031)
■ Awarded  ·  🇪🇸 Spain  ·  US$98m  ·  won by J. Vilella Construccions, S.l. / Highest Priority, S.l. / Construccion
This US$98m framework books the Spanish contractor group for dam civil works until 2031, which leaves outsiders little room until the work is re-tendered.
