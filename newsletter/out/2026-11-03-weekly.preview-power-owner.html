This week
Awards rose by six to 56 this week while new tenders fell by four to 30, so more work is being booked than put out to bid. China Communications Construction (CCCC) took three of those awards, led by the US$864m Annaba Port facilities contract in Algeria. For bidders, the open windows worth working are in European grid equipment: Západoslovenská distribučná wants three-phase oil power transformers by 4 December, and Skellefteå Kraft wants 400/130kV units by 16 January.
