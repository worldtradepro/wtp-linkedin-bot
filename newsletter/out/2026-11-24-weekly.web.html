This week
Awards rose to 97 from 77 while tenders held at 31, so more of the pipeline is closing than opening. The big open packages are European: HEP d.d.'s US$528m civil structures and equipment tender in Croatia and Infraestruturas de Portugal's US$396m ETCS signalling lot, due 14 Jan. At the small end, Chinese contractors sit in the winning teams on World Bank-financed power awards in India and Ethiopia. For bidders, the sizeable open money this week is EU civil and rail work; on small multilateral power packages, plan to team with or price against Chinese suppliers.
— World Trade Pro Research
