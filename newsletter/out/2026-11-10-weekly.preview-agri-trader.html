This week
Tenders slipped to 27 from 30 this week while awards edged up to 58. Most of the power hardware went to one supplier: Siemens Energy took the US$240m Austrian 380-220kV transformer award and the US$161m Łagisza turbine islands in Poland. Outside Europe, Chinese contractors won the US$416m Mazoon copper mining contract in Oman and a World Bank-funded dam in Uganda. If you buy transformers or turbines, qualify a second supplier early, because the market leader is filling its order book now.
