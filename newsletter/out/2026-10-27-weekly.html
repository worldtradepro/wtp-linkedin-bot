This week
EPC notices rose to 116 from 98 this week and tenders edged up to 34, while awards slipped to 49. One filer dominated the awards: China National Chemical Engineering (CNCEC) disclosed five contracts in a single filing, from a 300MW solar plant in Ghana to an FGL expansion in Algeria. The largest award was the US$403m Mechanical, Electrical and DSEAR Services contract in the UK. For bidders, the open work is in mid-sized European tenders, most of them closing in November, while CNCEC now has work committed on five jobs in three countries.
